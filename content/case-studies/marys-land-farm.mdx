<Chapter id="brief" number="01" title="A booking system shaped around the farm">

Mary's Land Farm brings together accommodation, venues, animals, activities and farm experiences. Customers need a way to book what they want to do, while staff need to manage those reservations and deliver everything included in each booking.

The project started with two challenges: an existing third-party SaaS booking solution and an early, rapidly assembled prototype. The farm wanted ownership of its code and a system built around its own requirements.

I was the sole engineer on the project, working with the team at Tempo Labs. I built the customer booking platform and the administration dashboard from scratch, covering product implementation, frontend and backend work through 2026. I used AI-assisted workflows throughout development.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="problems" number="02" title="Different bookings, one place to manage them">

The challenge was bringing the farm's different booking types into one system while preserving the rules that made each one work.

### One platform across rooms, venues and appointments

Each booking type had its own availability and pricing requirements. The customer experience needed to accommodate those differences, and staff needed a unified dashboard for managing reservations across them.

### A clear view of the day's operations

Administrators needed an availability calendar that showed which rooms, events and appointments were booked on each day, alongside the relevant customer details. This made the calendar an operational view of the farm's bookings.

### Tracking what customers had paid for

A reservation could include add-ons as well as the main booking. Staff needed to check customers in, see their purchases and manage those add-ons so they could track what had been paid for and what had been provided.

### Communication and access tied to the booking

Different appointment types needed different messages. Some reservations also required access to locked gates or spaces, so the system needed to connect the booking with the right email, SMS and access PIN.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="booking" number="03" title="The customer booking experience">

I built a customer platform for booking rooms, venues and farm appointments. Availability and pricing followed the farm's requirements for each booking type, with the goal of keeping the path from choosing an experience to completing a reservation straightforward.

Stripe handled payments. I also implemented tax calculations across the different appointment types and included add-ons in the booking experience, so customers could pay for the options associated with their visit.

The work involved connecting those business rules to the interface: showing availability, presenting the applicable price and collecting the information needed for the reservation.

<Chapter id="operations" number="04" title="An admin dashboard for the whole visit">

The admin dashboard brought booking management into one place. Staff could work across rooms, venues and appointments while keeping the specific rules for each type of reservation.

The availability calendar showed bookings by day with customer details, helping administrators understand what was booked and who was coming. Alongside that view, the dashboard supported customer check-in and add-on management.

This extended the system beyond taking a payment. Staff could track a customer's reservation and purchases through their visit, including the extras they had paid for and received.

The dashboard was highly customised to the farm's operating requirements, including the rules around availability, pricing, communication and access.

### From reservation to access PIN

For bookings that required access, the RemoteLock integration generated a PIN automatically. The system then sent that PIN to the customer by SMS according to the configuration set by an administrator.

### Messages suited to each appointment

I built custom email templates for specific appointment types. Together with SMS, this let the farm tailor booking communication to the experience a customer had reserved.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="frontend" number="06" title="Building the interface and supporting services">

The application used React and TypeScript, with Supabase and Postgres supporting the backend and data storage. Netlify hosted the web application.

I used shadcn/ui and established React accessibility patterns when building the interfaces. The customer platform focused on a clear booking process, while the admin dashboard organised the farm's more detailed operational controls.

As the sole engineer, I implemented the connected customer and administration experiences, including the backend work and external integrations needed to support them.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="results" number="07" title="A working platform for bookings and operations">

Before I left the project in 2026, the platform was recording almost $20,000 in monthly booking revenue and had around 1,000 active users using it to make bookings.

The delivered system combined a customer booking platform with an admin dashboard for reservations, availability, check-in and add-ons. Payments, email, SMS and physical access were integrated around the farm's requirements.

The project remained ongoing when my involvement ended. These figures describe the platform during my time on it.
